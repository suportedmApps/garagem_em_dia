# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: October 19, 2026**

## Introduction

Garagem em Dia ("the App") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your information.

This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the Garagem em Dia application.

Garagem em Dia is provided free of charge and is intended to be used as provided.

The App allows you to register and manage vehicles, organize maintenance records, track fuel consumption and refueling expenses, record general vehicle-related expenses, manage reminders, and view information about your vehicles to help you keep their maintenance and costs organized.

The App is designed to store information generated through your use of the App locally on your device, without requiring an account or registration.

The App may use third-party services, including Google AdMob, to display advertisements.

By using the App, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

---

## Information Storage

Garagem em Dia is designed to store information generated through your use of the App locally on your device.

Depending on the features you use, information stored locally may include:

* Vehicle names and descriptions
* Vehicle makes, models, years, types, and license plate information
* Current vehicle mileage and other vehicle details
* Maintenance records, including service descriptions, dates, costs, mileage, workshops, and notes
* Fueling records, including fuel types, dates, liters, prices, total costs, mileage, gas stations, and notes
* General expense records, including descriptions, categories, amounts, dates, establishments, and notes
* Reminder descriptions, due dates, mileage limits, and completion status
* App preferences, settings, and other information generated by the App

This information is used to provide the App's vehicle-management, maintenance-tracking, expense-management, and reminder functionality.

The App does not require an account or registration to use its core features.

Information entered into the App is stored locally on your device and is not routinely transmitted to or stored on servers operated by the developer.

The developer does not have direct access to information stored locally within the App.

Core features that rely on locally stored information are designed to work without requiring an Internet connection. Internet access may be required for third-party services, such as advertising.

---

## Personal Information

Garagem em Dia does not require you to provide personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, or home address to use its core features.

However, the App allows you to enter vehicle-related information, which may include license plate numbers, vehicle details, maintenance information, and other notes.

Some of this information may be associated with you or another identifiable person, depending on what you choose to enter.

The developer does not independently collect or maintain this locally entered information on its own servers.

You should avoid entering sensitive personal information into vehicle descriptions, maintenance notes, expense records, or other fields unless necessary for your own use.

---

## Vehicle Information and User-Generated Content

Garagem em Dia allows you to create, edit, organize, and manage vehicle information and related records.

User-generated content may include:

* Vehicle identification and descriptive information
* Vehicle mileage and usage-related information
* Maintenance history and service details
* Fueling history and fuel-related expenses
* General expenses associated with vehicles
* Reminder settings and maintenance deadlines
* Notes and other information voluntarily entered into the App

This information is used to provide the App's core features, including displaying vehicle histories, organizing expenses, monitoring maintenance activities, and managing reminders.

Vehicle records and related information are stored locally on your device and are not uploaded to or maintained on servers operated by the developer as part of the App's core functionality.

If the App is uninstalled or its local storage is cleared, locally stored information may be deleted. The availability of any recovery options depends on the backup mechanisms available on your device and whether you have independently created a backup.

## Notifications and Reminders

Garagem em Dia may use notifications to remind you about scheduled maintenance, upcoming deadlines, mileage-related reminders, or other events configured within the App.

Notifications are intended to provide functionality requested or configured by the user.

Information required to schedule and manage local notifications may be stored or managed locally on your device.

The App does not need to send your vehicle records, maintenance history, or expense history to the developer's servers to provide local notifications.

Notification delivery may depend on your device settings, operating system restrictions, notification permissions, and other technical conditions.

If you disable notifications through the App or your device settings, some reminder functionality may no longer be available.

## Data Retention

Information entered into Garagem em Dia is generally retained locally on your device until you delete it, clear the App's storage, uninstall the App, or otherwise remove the information.

## Data Deletion

Garagem em Dia stores user-generated information, including vehicle details, maintenance records, fueling history, expenses, and reminders, locally on the user's device.

Because the developer does not maintain a server-side account or database containing this information, the developer cannot directly delete information stored locally on your device.

To delete information stored by Garagem em Dia, you can:

* Delete individual vehicles or records through the App, where the relevant functionality is available.
* Use any data-clearing or deletion functionality provided within the App, if available.
* Clear the App's storage or data through your Android device settings.
* Uninstall Garagem em Dia from your device.

These actions may permanently remove locally stored information associated with the App installation. Before clearing the App's storage or uninstalling it, make sure you have saved any information you wish to retain.

Information processed independently by third-party services, such as Google AdMob, is subject to the respective providers' privacy policies and available privacy controls.

If you have questions about information processed by a third-party provider, you should consult that provider's privacy policy or contact the provider directly.
